Coverage: what each Massachusetts source publishes

This page exists so that any figure on this site can be checked against its source. It lists all 59 Massachusetts municipal sources in the dataset, how many records each contributes, the span they cover, and - just as important - what each one leaves out.

What we do not have

Stated positively, because it governs which questions this data can answer: 40 of 59 sources publish no declared project value at all, so no cost figure can exist for those towns. 16 publish category labels rather than descriptions of the work, which is why they are excluded from every article that depends on reading what the job was. Only 8 publish coordinates.

Monthly cadence. Amherst, Barnstable, Bourne, Chatham, Lexington, Plymouth, Provincetown, Reading, Sudbury, Truro, Wakefield, Waltham, Wareham publish on a monthly schedule rather than continuously, so their most recent weeks are always behind the rest of the dataset.
